Flight Profile Jetayu - MK4

Conventional Limitations

Energy & Endurance

Limited range and heavy battery constraints limit operational viability.

Operational Safety

Vulnerabilities resulting from exposed rotor blades and FOD hazards.

Acoustic Signature

High-frequency noise impacts stealth operations and urban use.

Systemic Inefficiency

Massive aerodynamic losses in traditional quadcopter airframes.

Jetayu Innovations

APU Regeneration

In-flight regeneration during the cruise phase to maximize endurance.

Propulsion Redundancy

Combined electric and fluidic systems ensure mission-critical redundancy in contested airspace.

Aero-Acoustics

Integrated acoustic liners and internal ducted architecture significantly reduce disc loading and tonal noise.

Adaptive Morphing

Morphing wings actively adjust and stow to eliminate vertical drag during VTOL flight phases.

Applications of JETAYU

Remote Lifting & Logistics

Precision slung-loading in rugged terrain. Capable of transporting pre-fab modules up to 150 KG payloads at 15,000 FT altitudes.

High-Altitude Logistics

Autonomous cargo delivery in low-density altitudes. Operating up to 18,000 FT in extreme cold (-4°C), supporting loads up to 150 KG.

Offshore Utility Support

Critical replacement part delivery to offshore oil and wind platforms, maintaining operational continuity. Max payload of 150 KG.

Disaster Relief Cargo Drops

Rapid deployment of essential aid post-catastrophe, air-dropping up to 150 KG of food, water, and medical supplies into inaccessible zones.
